Tiny House: a wooden cabin for two by the water

2 guests · 15 m² · Pulligny

Book on AirbnbBook on Booking.com

Our Tiny House is a small, well-insulated 15 m² wooden cabin set in the garden facing the river. Everything is designed for a couple or a solo traveller: a proper double bed, a full kitchen and a modern shower room. Let the real sounds of nature lull you to sleep.

Amenities

  • 1 double bed
  • Equipped kitchen: hob, oven, hood, microwave, fridge
  • Shower room with toilet
  • TV
  • Fast Wi-Fi
  • Garden and river view
  • Private parking (cars and motorbikes)
  • River access for fishing

Frequently asked questions

Can I fish on site?

Yes, the garden opens directly onto the Madon river. You need a fishing licence valid in Meurthe-et-Moselle, available online at cartedepeche.fr.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is private parking on site for cars and motorbikes.

Can I arrive late in the evening?

Yes, late arrivals are possible. Just let us know your arrival time.

How do I book?

Book on Airbnb or Booking.com using the buttons on each rental page, or contact us directly by phone or e-mail.
