Studio Perché: a terrace above the river, for up to four

4 guests · Pulligny

Book on AirbnbBook on Booking.com

The Studio Perché ("perched studio") takes up the upper floor of the house, with its own entrance via a spiral staircase. From the terrace you look out over the garden and the river. The open-plan living area with kitchen and desk suits families and remote workers alike.

Amenities

  • Bedroom with king-size bed
  • Sofa bed in the living area
  • Baby cot available
  • Equipped kitchen: hob, oven, microwave, fridge
  • Shower room with hydromassage shower
  • Desk for remote work
  • Fast Wi-Fi
  • Terrace with river view
  • Private entrance
  • Private parking

Frequently asked questions

Can I fish on site?

Yes, the garden opens directly onto the Madon river. You need a fishing licence valid in Meurthe-et-Moselle, available online at cartedepeche.fr.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is private parking on site for cars and motorbikes.

Can I arrive late in the evening?

Yes, late arrivals are possible. Just let us know your arrival time.

How do I book?

Book on Airbnb or Booking.com using the buttons on each rental page, or contact us directly by phone or e-mail.
